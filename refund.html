Refund, Dispute & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

1. Overview

Freedom Living Inc is committed to ensuring client satisfaction with our services. This policy outlines the procedures for refunds, disputes, and cancellations related to paid programs and services.

2. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your enrollment in any paid program or service under the following conditions:

  • Before program start: Full refund if cancellation is submitted at least 14 days before the program start date
  • Within 7 days of start: 50% refund if cancellation is submitted within the first 7 days of program commencement
  • After 7 days: No refund will be issued for cancellations made more than 7 days after the program has begun

To cancel, submit a written request to support@freedomlivinginc.com with your name, program name, and enrollment date.

3. Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed as follows:

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Processing takes 10–15 business days from the date of approval
  • You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated
  • Bank processing times may vary and are outside our control

4. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Administrative and registration fees
  • Materials and resources that have been accessed or downloaded
  • Completed services or sessions already delivered
  • Donation-based contributions

5. Disputes

If you believe you have been incorrectly charged or are dissatisfied with a service, we encourage you to resolve the matter directly with us before pursuing other channels:

  • Step 1: Contact our support team at support@freedomlivinginc.com with a detailed description of the issue
  • Step 2: Our team will review your case and respond within 5 business days
  • Step 3: If the dispute is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may request an escalation to management for further review

6. Exceptions

Freedom Living Inc reserves the right to make exceptions to this policy on a case-by-case basis, particularly in circumstances involving documented hardship or service delivery issues on our part.

7. Contact

For refund requests, cancellations, or disputes, please contact us at support@freedomlivinginc.com.
